# Auto Insurance Data Analysis Dashboards

This repository contains two interactive dashboards built using IBM Cognos Analytics, created from the Customer Analysis sample dataset. The dashboards explore state-wise insurance sales targets and auto insurance claim 
dynamics, offering valuable insights into customer trends across the United States.

**Dashboards Overview**

**1. Statewise Insurance Targets & Vehicle Class Analysis**

<ins>Objective</ins> : Analyze sales targets for auto insurance across different U.S. states and vehicle classes.

**Key Features:**

* Geographic distribution of customers, with states like Arizona, California, Nevada, Washington, Oregon highlighted.

* Vehicle class categorization into Luxury Cars, Sports Cars, SUVs, Luxury SUVs, and Two/Four-Door Cars.

* Aggregated sales target metrics (e.g., 11.6K) represented clearly for decision-making.

<ins>Purpose</ins> : Provides a high-level view of where insurance sales are concentrated and how different vehicle categories contribute to targets.

**2. Auto Insurance Claim Dynamics by State & Vehicle**

<ins>Objective</ins> : Examine the dynamics of insurance claims across states and vehicle categories.

**Key Features:**

* State-level classification for insurance claims (e.g., Oregon, Arizona, California).

* Visualization of max and min claim amounts across states and vehicle classes.

* Claim insights for various vehicle categories such as Two-Door Cars, SUVs, Luxury Cars, Luxury SUVs, Sports Cars, Four-Door Cars.

Example: Maximum claim amount observed is 2,345.41, while the minimum is 55.51.

<ins>Purpose</ins> : Helps identify states and vehicle types associated with higher/lower claim amounts, useful for risk analysis and strategy planning.

**Tools & Dataset**

-> Tool Used: IBM Cognos Analytics

-> Dataset: Customer Analysis (sample dataset provided within IBM Cognos)

 * Visual Elements:

-> Map-based geographic analysis

-> Bubble charts for vehicle class distribution

-> KPI cards for claim amounts and targets

**Insights Gained**

States like Arizona and Oregon show distinct insurance patterns in both targets and claims.

Vehicle class significantly influences both sales performance and claim dynamics.

The dashboards provide a dual perspective:

 * Sales-focused view - understanding targets and customer distribution.

 * Claims-focused view -  analyzing financial exposure and risks.

**Conclusion**

These dashboards highlight how data visualization can uncover patterns in insurance sales and claims across states and vehicle categories. By leveraging IBM Cognos Analytics,this projectdemonstrates how business
users and analysts can turn raw data into actionable insights for decision-making in the insurance domain.
